# Contributing to Snapmaker U1 Community Hub

Contributions, improvements, and feedback from the community are warmly welcome!

---

## 💡 Ways to Contribute

1. **Filament Presets & Slicer Profiles:**
   - Submit new tested filament presets or suggestions for existing profiles via [GitHub Issues](../../issues/new?template=profile-feedback.yml) or Pull Requests.
   - Categorization: New profiles are tagged as `community-submitted`, `tested`, or `experimental`.

2. **Dashboard Tweaks & Bug Reports:**
   - Notice a styling quirk on your mobile screen or a Klipper macro update? Open an issue or PR with the proposed YAML change.

3. **Translations & Documentation:**
   - Help improve English or German documentation or propose guides for community mods.

---

## 📜 Pull Request Guidelines

- Keep PRs concise and focused on a single topic.
- Do not check in private tokens, personal LAN IPs (use placeholders like `192.168.x.x`), or camera streams.
- Respect open-source licenses: All code is released under the **MIT License**.
