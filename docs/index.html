Operator Guide & FAQ

Essential cheat sheets for loading, unloading, and daily handling

📤

Unloading Filament

In a nutshell: Auto-heat → Hand-reel spool
With Dashboard:

Click toolhead (T0–T3) → select Unload. Hotend heats up, motor unlocks, reel filament by hand.

Touchscreen:

Select Filament → Unload → pick toolhead. Wait for nozzle temperature prompt, then gently pull.

💡 Why does it have slight resistance?

Light tactile resistance is completely normal as the filament passes the feeder gears. Pull smoothly! To remove the spool: hold the holder arm at the chassis and pull the spool straight outward.

📥

Loading Filament

In a nutshell: 45° cut → Push to feeder → Press Load
1. Cut 45°:

Snip filament cleanly at a 45° angle. Hang spool on the spindle.

2. Insert:

Push filament into the feed tube until the inlet sensor trips (Dashboard shows: At feeder only).

3. Trigger Load:

Click Load in Dashboard or Touchscreen. The printer preheats, motor feeds through the tube, and purges a test bead.

💡 Pro Tip for smooth feeding

Never insert bent or flattened filament tips. A sharp 45° cut ensures the filament slides effortlessly through the toolchanger dock inlet.

What does 'At feeder only' mean in the dashboard?
The filament has been inserted into the Bowden feed inlet and the inlet sensor confirmed its presence, but it has not been advanced through the long tube to the nozzle yet. Simply tap Load to let the motor feed it to the hotend.
Does the motor automatically spit out the filament during unload?
No, and that is intentional by design! The extruder motor only pulls the hot tip back out of the melt zone to prevent thick bulbs from jamming the PTFE tube. The long distance through the tube back to the spool is reeled up smoothly by hand to prevent messy tangles.
Can I pull filament out when the nozzle is cold?
Never! Solidified plastic inside the cold nozzle will tear or damage the feeder mechanism if pulled with force. Always allow the nozzle to reach at least 200°C (PLA) or 240°C (PETG) before retracting.
When should I use 'Cancel Print' vs. Emergency Stop?
Cancel Print: Standard graceful stop. Finishes current move, raises Z-axis, safely docks active toolhead, and turns off heaters.
Emergency Stop: Immediate power cut to all motors and heaters. Use only in case of physical collision or risk of damage. Requires Klipper firmware restart afterward.

Installation Guide

Follow these 3 simple steps to get your dashboard and profiles running.

1

Prerequisites & Custom Cards

Ensure HACS is installed. Install these required Lovelace cards from HACS:

  • button-card
  • card-mod
  • slider-entity-row
  • vertical-stack-in-card
  • mini-graph-card
  • tabbed-card
  • fold-entity-row
2

Copy Showcase Assets

Download and place the following files in your Home Assistant /config/www/snapmaker-u1/showcase/ directory:

u1-fusion-showcase.u1.js studio.png close-up.png

Then register the resource in Home Assistant under Settings → Dashboards → Resources as JavaScript Module with URL: /local/snapmaker-u1/showcase/u1-fusion-showcase.u1.js.

3

Import Dashboard YAML

Create a new Lovelace dashboard (or edit an existing one), open the Raw Configuration Editor, and paste the configuration from homeassistant/u1_dashboard_complete.yaml.

Click here to view / copy quick import instructions

You can also import views individually: views/snapmaker-u1.yaml, views/u1-minimal.yaml, and views/u1-maintenance.yaml.
