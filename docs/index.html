Automations, Slicer Profiles & Hardware Mod

Beyond the dashboard itself, this repository ships ready-to-use automations, a complete OrcaSlicer preset suite, and a tested chamber airflow upgrade.

🌬️

Cool after printing (cavity_fan)

Compact toggle & slider panel right below Part cooling fan: cools the PEI bed after completion (default 5 min at 70 %) and stops automatically at ≤ 36 °C or next print. Included in u1_control_package.yaml or standalone.

Open YAML & settings →
🎛️

9 OrcaSlicer Process Profiles (0.4 & 0.6 mm)

Standardized profiles for every job: Balanced (0.20 / 0.28 mm, VFA-free 200 mm/s), Fast for organizers, Functional for brackets, Draft for rapid fit checks, plus QR-Code & Schild (0.4 mm).

Browse all 9 process profiles →
🧵

17 Verified Filament Presets & LAN Machines

Tuned flow, pressure advance, and volumetric limits for 9 materials (SUNLU PLA+ 2.0, Bambu PLA/PETG, ELEGOO PLA Matte & PETG-CF, eSUN PETG Matte, GEEETECH PLA/TPU 95A, JAYO HS PETG) for 0.4 & 0.6 mm nozzles + LAN printer profiles.

Download filament presets →
🌀

Hardware Mod: Auxiliary Fan Deflector

U1-Fan_Deflector-Smooth.stl clips onto the side AUX fan and redirects horizontal airflow upward into the chamber — stopping left-bed PLA/PETG warping so 30 % AUX cooling stays safely usable.

View STL & print guide →

Completion Chime & Light Pulse

Optional fertigdruck_fanfare.yaml: pulses the chamber light for 1.2 s on entry into the final layer (visible even with auto light-off enabled) and triggers an optional Alexa bell_02 ding.
