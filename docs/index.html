Home Assistant

Snapmaker U1 Dashboard

Manage filament and temperatures, monitor the print and check maintenance in Home Assistant. The interactive printer card gives you a shortcut to the controls.

A dashboard by Reaper2507, built on Ironoxx’s Snapmaker U1 integration. See the credits.

See the full dashboardInstallation
Tap the printer to see its controls
  • Filament and live spool colors See loaded colors at T0–T3, assign spools, switch between loaded tools, and start loading or unloading.
  • Nozzles and heated bed Read current and target temperatures and adjust supported setpoints in Filament & heat.
  • Monitor and control prints Check camera, layer and remaining time. Pause a print or adjust speed and flow when supported.
  • Maintenance and diagnostics Check service intervals, temperature history, bed mesh and system status.

Three dashboard views

Dashboard

Printer controls, filament management, camera and layer view.

Minimal

A compact view of the current job, live camera and time remaining.

Maintenance & Diagnostics

Service checklist, temperature history, bed mesh and self-test.
