Community Hub
  • Studio & Cutaway layouts: Clear view with T0–T3 spools, live XYZ & temperatures, and Controls & Filament & Heat popups.
  • Three dashboard views + control package: Dashboard, Minimal, and Maintenance & Diagnostics paired with u1_control_package.yaml.

Dashboard (Main View)

Top: printer graphic with T0–T3 spools, camera with light toggle, and job card. Below: speed, flow, Cooling after printing, all four toolheads, and axis controls.

Cooling after printing

Switch below Part cooling fan: when On, the side fan runs after printing finishes until the bed reaches 36 °C (or time runs out). Tapping the row opens Time and Fan speed sliders. YAML →

Minimal View

Compact daily view without the full head and motion panels: printer graphic, camera, last print, Emergency stop, Cooling after printing, files, and layer view.

Files, History & Layer View

Start prints directly from the list, browse timelapses, and skip individual objects mid-print using Ironoxx’s live layer view.

Maintenance & Diagnostics

Klipper G-code console, temperature graph, 11×11 bed mesh, system diagnostics, and maintenance counters on one page.

Tap the Printer → Controls & Heat

Tapping the printer card opens speed, flow, pause, and per-head filament and temperature controls.

Studio / Cutaway Toggle

Optional Cutaway graphic showing live X/Y/Z coordinates and which toolhead is mounted or parked. Preview →
